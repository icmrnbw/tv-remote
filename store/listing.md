## English (en-US)

**Title**

    Remote for Rosso & Android TV

**Short description**

    Free Wi‑Fi remote for Android TV, Google TV and Rosso smart TVs.

**Full description**

    Turn your phone into a remote for your TV. No IR blaster needed: it works over your Wi‑Fi, or over Bluetooth.

    WHAT IT CONTROLS
    • Android TV and Google TV (TVs and Google TV / Android TV sticks, including Rosso Android models): full control. Pair once with the code shown on the TV.
    • Other Smart TVs, including many Rosso Linux models: some functions only, usually volume, mute, and opening YouTube and Netflix. What works depends on what your TV allows.
    • Bluetooth mode (experimental): for TVs that accept a Bluetooth keyboard or remote, the phone pairs with the TV as a Bluetooth remote. Needs Android 9 or newer.

    BUTTONS
    Power, input, mute, settings, arrows and OK, back, home, menu, volume and channel (hold to repeat), play/pause, rewind and fast forward, number pad, guide, info, and shortcuts to YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+.

    HOW TO START
    1. Connect the phone and the TV to the same Wi‑Fi.
    2. Open the app; it finds TVs on your network. Tap yours, or enter the TV's IP address.
    3. On Android TV, type the 6‑character code from the TV screen.

    Your TV isn't supported? Use “Check this TV” in the app and send the report to the developer so support can be added.

    In English, Russian and Uzbek. Free, with ads.
